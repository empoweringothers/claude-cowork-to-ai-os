# Start Here

This is a private AI OS recovered from selected Claude Cowork material.

## Read in this order

1. `PRIVACY.md` — what may be read, processed, or shared.
2. `OBJECTIVE.md` and `MOTIVES.md` — the result and purpose, after review.
3. `HOME.md` — the map of recovered projects and source material.
4. `STATE.md` — current, human-approved work only.
5. The relevant project under `Projects/`.

## Recovery status

Imported chats, memory, files, and instructions begin as source material. They
do not become current facts or live instructions until a person reviews and
promotes them.

Start with `Inbox/REVIEW.md`. The initial integrity record is
`.ai-os/manifests/scaffold.json`; run `cowork-ai-os verify "<this-ai-os>"` and
keep its report outside this folder unless you deliberately copy in a reviewed
version.
